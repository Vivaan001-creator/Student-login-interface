Refund Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy covers tuition fees paid through GenAlpha. Fees are paid directly to the tutor by UPI.

1. When we refund

  • Duplicate or wrong payment: if you paid twice, or paid more than the amount shown in the app, we refund the extra amount.
  • Classes we cancel: if the tutor cancels classes that you have already paid for and does not hold them later, we refund or adjust that part of the fee.
  • Enrolment that does not go ahead: if a student does not start, we refund the fee paid in advance.

2. When we do not refund

  • Classes that were held, even if the student was absent.
  • A student who leaves partway through a month already attended.

3. How to ask

Email vivaan510399@gmail.com with the student's roll number, the date and amount paid, and the UPI reference. We check the payment record and reply with our decision.

4. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are sent to the same UPI account or bank account the payment came from, within 7 working days of approval.

5. Payments reported in the app

A payment you report shows as pending until the tutor verifies it. If it cannot be matched to a bank or UPI record it is rejected, and nothing has been charged by GenAlpha. Any money you sent to the tutor is handled under this policy.
