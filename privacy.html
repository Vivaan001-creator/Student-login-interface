Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

GenAlpha is a home tuition management service run by Vivaan, a private tutor. This page explains what personal data the GenAlpha website and app collect, why, who can see it, and how you can ask us to correct or delete it.

Contact for anything on this page: vivaan510399@gmail.com. This address is also our grievance contact for data protection requests.

1. What we collect and why

We only collect what is needed to run tuition classes: keeping attendance, recording marks, sharing homework and notices, and tracking fees.

Whose dataWhat we collectWhy
StudentName, roll number, class and section, date of birth, father's name, parent contact number, optional photoTo identify the student, let the student or parent sign in, and contact the family
Student recordsAttendance, marks and results, homework, fee records and payment reportsTo run classes and show progress to the student and parent
ParentContact number, alternate mobile number, email address, and the payment reports and profile change requests you sendTo link a parent to their children, record fee payments, and send notices. Profile changes are reviewed by the tutor before they are applied

Everyone who signs inSign-in time, role, name, classTo show the admin who has used the app and to spot unusual activity

We do not collect location, contacts, microphone, camera or device identifiers. We do not use advertising or analytics tools.

2. Children's data

Most students are under 18. Student records are created by the tutor only after a parent or guardian has agreed to enrol the child, and a parent or guardian can ask us at any time to show, correct or delete the child's record. We do not track or monitor children's behaviour for any purpose other than their own tuition (attendance, marks and homework), and we never show advertising to anyone.

3. Who can see the data

  • The tutor (admin) can see all records.
  • A teacher can see the students and classes assigned to them.
  • A student or parent can see that student's own records and the general notices.

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for marketing.

4. Service providers we rely on

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage) stores account details, records and uploaded photos. Google may process this data on servers outside India.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) or your email provider carries an admission request if you send one.
  • Google Fonts and Cloudflare cdnjs (for icons) deliver fonts and icons. When a page loads, these providers receive your IP address and browser details, as with any website that uses them.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it.

5. How long we keep data

We keep a student's record while the student is enrolled and for as long afterwards as is needed for the tuition service, fee records, or a legal requirement. After that we delete it or remove anything that identifies the student. You can ask for earlier deletion at any time.

6. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS and access is limited by sign-in. No online service can promise perfect security. If a breach affects your data we will tell you and report it as the law requires.

7. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you (or a parent or guardian for a child) can:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold and how it is used;
  • ask us to correct or complete it;
  • ask us to delete it, or withdraw consent for its use;
  • make a complaint about how it is handled.

Email us from the address we have on record, or tell the tutor in person. We will answer within the time the law allows. If you are not satisfied with our answer you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Notifications, cookies and browser storage

If you tap Yes to notifications, your browser keeps that permission and the app shows alerts about new notices and app updates on that device. We do not collect or store any push tokens or device identifiers for this.

The site does not set tracking cookies. See the Cookies Policy for the small items stored in your browser.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. If the change is significant we will tell parents and teachers directly.
