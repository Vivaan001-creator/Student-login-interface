Cookies Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

GenAlpha does not set advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, so there is no cookie banner to accept. The app does keep a few small items in your browser so that it works.

ItemPurposeHow long
Sign-in status and your roll number, contact number or teacher IDKeeps you signed in and opens the right recordsUntil you log out
Firebase sign-in session (browser storage)Keeps teachers and the admin signed in securelyUntil you log out
Theme choice (light or dark)Remembers your display preferenceUntil you clear browser data
Notification choice, and which notices and app updates you have already seenRemembers whether you said Yes or No to notifications, and shows a red dot only for new itemsUntil you clear browser data
Last login timeShows you when you last used the appUntil you log out

Third parties

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and icons from Cloudflare cdnjs. These providers can see your IP address when your browser requests those files. We do not embed videos, maps, social media buttons or advertising.

Your choices

You can clear this storage at any time in your browser settings or by logging out. If you do, you will need to sign in again.

More detail is in the Privacy Policy.
